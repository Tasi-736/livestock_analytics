How the classification works

Production is labelled High when it is at or above the median of the cleaned provincial records, and Low otherwise. The split is 64% training, 16% validation, and 20% final testing. SMOTENC is attempted only when a model's validation accuracy is below 80%, and synthetic rows are retained only when validation accuracy improves.
